Politics & Satire

Etatisti No. I is German-language satire punk about politics in Germany: the coalition agreement as a circus contract (Zirkusvertrag), Bärbel Bas (Wir brauchen Bas), Lars Klingbeil (Klingbeils Kernschmelze) and the ECB as the sorcerer's apprentice (Zauberlehrling 2.0 – EZB oWEHoWEH). Fast, sharp and always with the arithmetic behind the promises.

Made for

  • German-speaking fans of political satire and cabaret
  • People who follow German politics – coalition, budget, ECB
  • Fans of German punk with a message
