Privacy Policy – Etatisti Autoloop

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Etatisti Autoloop is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to, and manage, the Etatisti YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

The app uses Google OAuth to upload videos and edit video metadata on the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not access data of any other Google user.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's private server. They are not shared with or sold to third parties.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies of its own. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
