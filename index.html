About

Etatisti is brainpunk — texts to think about, music to enjoy. By accident you might understand or learn something — and where a song is a bad example, it teaches how not to do things. Warning label: may contain portions of sarcasm. Usually more than portions.

The songs carry real cargo: the four laws of thermodynamics, Kirchhoff's circuit rules, the energy return on a barrel of oil, the arithmetic behind political promises. Every lyric is written and fact-checked by a human who runs real machines and real balance sheets by day and spends the night wondering why politics keeps voting against physics. The sound is rendered with AI tools and honestly labeled as such — the machine is the instrument, never the author.

Stylistically, Etatisti refuses to stay in one lane. Big beat next to ballad, industrial stomp next to Italian sunshine pop, electro-swing next to a whispered outro — whatever the message needs, the spectrum delivers. Albums arrive in several languages — English, German, Italian, Swedish — because facts do not stop at borders, and neither should the jokes.

Fronting it all: three avatars under an aurora sky — a marble David for stone-cold reason, a chrome cyborg for the machine between us, a crowned Cleopatra for desire and power. They sing about entropy, energy and the occasional minister, and they never pretend fiction is fact.

If a song makes you laugh, check the footnotes: it is probably true. Facts, not fiction. Time is of the essence — make it count.
