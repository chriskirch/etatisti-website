Eyes & Vision

Time to FOCUS is a punk EP about eye care: Ortho-K going wild, sclerals as the gateway to focus, RGP as the lens to be, a song about myopia and D.E.D – dry eye disease as the curse of digital misuse. Five tracks in English.

Made for

  • Optometrists, opticians and contact lens specialists
  • Ophthalmologists, orthoptists and optometry students
  • Parents of short-sighted (myopic) children
  • Keratoconus patients and wearers of scleral, RGP or Ortho-K lenses
  • Anyone with dry eyes from too much screen time
